# Design manual Rev I · 01 Loads and criteria

UATXcavators DN600 slurry microTBM (micro tunnel boring machine). Revision I, 2026-10-28. Generated by `tools/make_manual.py` from the model and calculation scripts in the repo; edit those, not this file.

Units mm, N, MPa (kN, kNm in tables). x = 0 at the ring 1 front face, +x rearward. Clock positions are seen from the front, looking rearward: 0° = 12 o'clock, 90° = 3 o'clock.

Every check in this manual uses one of the load cases below, the allowables in section 1.7 and the required factors of safety in section 1.8. Source: `src/loads.py`, `src/calc_revI.py`.

## 1.1 Thrust from the hydraulics

The four jacking cylinders (Chief WT 5 × 24, bore 127.0) push on the thrust ring. The total force for a cylinder pressure p is

```
F = n · p · A_bore = 4 × p × (π/4 × 127.0²) = 4 × p × 12,668 mm²
```

Each design case is the force at one pressure setting of the thrust circuit, with the protections that must fail to reach it:

| Case | Pressure | F | What has to fail to get there |
|---|---|---|---|
| LC1 | thrust model | 303 kN | Nothing: normal boring, factored lubricated jacking demand at 5 m (Thrust_Model, Scenarios) |
| LC2 | 121 bar | 613.1 kN | Nothing: the thrust-circuit reducing valve caps the cylinders at 121 bar; a jam at the cap is the credible worst case |
| LC3 | 158 bar | 800.6 kN | The reducing valve; the jack-pressure interlock stops the pump at 158 bar. Structural design case |
| LC4 | 177.9 bar (2,580 psi) | 901.4 kN | The reducing valve and the interlock; the pump relief caps the pressure. Hardware limit |

## 1.2 Moment from an off-centre jam

A jam rarely loads the face evenly. The thrust resultant is placed at e = R/3 = 315/3 = 105 mm off the axis (the team's jam screen, 590 kN with 62 kNm, uses the same eccentricity). The weight of the cantilevered module, head and drive adds M_g at the ring 1 seat (section 1.5):

```
M = F · e + M_g          LC3: 800.6 kN × 0.105 m + 3.05 kNm = 87.11 kNm
```

LC1 (normal boring) uses e/2 = 52.5 mm.

## 1.3 Side force from an uneven torque reaction (added in Rev I)

When only part of the face resists the bits, the tangential cutting forces do not cancel and leave a net side force on the head. For resistance on half the face (a jam), with friction-like tangential traction:

```
F_s = 3 T / (π R)                        half-face contact
F_s = 2 T e / R²                          linear pressure p0 (1 + k r cosθ / R), LC1 with e = 52.5
LC2-LC4: T = stall torque at the pump relief = 11.58 kNm  →  F_s = 3 × 11.58e6 / (π × 315) = 35.1 kN
```

F_s acts at the cutting edges (x = -215) and at 90° to the eccentricity, so its moment adds to F·e as a vector:

```
M(x) = √[ (F e + M_g)² + (F_s (x − x_face))² ]      at the seat, LC3: √[87.11² + (35.1 × 0.245)²] = 87.54 kNm
```

The vector sum adds 0.5 % to the seat moment; F_s itself loads the front bearing (chapter 03).

| Case | Pressure (bar) | F (kN) | e (mm) | F·e + M_g (kNm) | F_s (kN) | M at the seat (kNm) | T (kNm) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LC1 | thrust model | 303.0 | 52.5 | 18.96 | 6.3 | 19.02 | 6 | normal boring, factored lubricated demand at 5 m (Thrust_Model) |
| LC2 | 121.0 | 613.1 | 105.0 | 67.43 | 35.1 | 67.97 | 20 | jam at the 121 bar thrust cap |
| LC3 | 158.0 | 800.6 | 105.0 | 87.11 | 35.1 | 87.54 | 20 | jam at the 158 bar jack-pressure interlock (reducing valve failed) |
| LC4 | 177.9 | 901.4 | 105.0 | 97.69 | 35.1 | 98.07 | 20 | jam at the 2,580 psi pump relief (valve and interlock failed) |

## 1.4 Torque

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Motor displacement | 2,812 cm³/rev → 44.71 Nm/bar | Poclain MS18 catalogue, theoretical |
| Torque interlock | 10 kNm | Control system stops rotation above 10 kNm |
| Operating pressure at the interlock | p_op = T / (V_g η) = 10,000 / (44.71 × 0.95) = 235.4 bar | η = 0.95 mechanical efficiency |
| Pump relief (rules 4.c.3.2) | p_op + max(10 %, 20 bar) = 235.4 + 23.5 = 259.0 bar | "10 % or 20 bar, whichever is higher, above operating pressure" |
| Stall torque at the relief | 44.71 × 259.0 = 11.58 kNm | Largest torque the motor can apply |
| Structural design torque | T_d = 20 kNm = 2 × interlock | Used for torque-path parts (it exceeds the stall torque by 1.73×) |
| Motor maximum pressure | 400 bar → 17.88 kNm | Only if the relief fails; below T_d |
| LC1 operating torque | 6 kNm | Thrust_Model demand |

## 1.6 Chamber pressure

The slurry chamber runs at 0.2-1.0 bar. The top of the window acts on the whole bulkhead area:

```
P_ch = p · π/4 · D_shell,ID² = 0.1 MPa × π/4 × 584.2² = 26.8 kN
```

## 1.8 Required factors of safety

The rules (3.b.1.1) ask for a factor of safety "appropriate for the structure": 1.5 on a main shell, more than 5 on lifting, 3-10 common in TBMs. Rev I uses:

| Item | LC1-LC3 | LC4 (two protections failed) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand checks of structural parts | FoS ≥ 1.0 on the ASD allowable (≥ 1.67 on yield in bending, ≥ 1.44 in shear) | FoS ≥ 1.0 on the allowable | AISC ASD |
| FEA, membrane + bending (linearized) | FoS ≥ 1.5 on yield | FoS ≥ 1.0 on yield | Rules example for a main shell |
| FEA peak stress at a notch | FoS ≥ 1.0 on yield (local) | reported | Peaks are local; they govern fatigue, not static strength of ductile steel |
| Bolted joints | Clamp ≥ 1.5 × the force needed to keep the joint closed; slip FoS ≥ 1.0 at μ 0.15, dowels as backup | | VDI 2230 practice |
| Bearings | s0 ≥ 2 at LC2-LC3 (shock loads) | s0 ≥ 1.5 | SKF guidance |
| Catalogue parts (locking assemblies, coupling) | Rating ≥ demand at T_d = 20 kNm | | Manufacturer ratings |
| Lifting | Hoist ring WLL ≥ load (WLL carries 5:1 on breaking); welded attachments FoS ≥ 3 on yield and ≥ 5 on ultimate | | Rules 3.b.1.1, ASME B30.26 |

## 1.9 FEA method

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Geometry | CadQuery solids built from the same constants as the model (`src/fea_ring1.py`, `src/fea_thrust_ring.py`) |
| Mesh | gmsh, 10-node (quadratic) tetrahedra; 3-3.5 mm at notches and ligaments, 9-14 mm elsewhere; 0.4-0.7 million degrees of freedom |
| Solver | scikit-fem linear elasticity, E 200 GPa, ν 0.3; algebraic multigrid (pyamg) with rigid-body modes, conjugate gradients to 1e-8 |
| Symmetry | Ring 1: half model (z ≥ 0). Thrust ring: quarter model |
| Ring 1 loads | Seat pressure p = a + b·y on the counterbore floor, scaled so the resultant is exactly F + P_ch over the counterbore and the moment is exactly M(seat), with 12 o'clock (the pipe holes) most compressed; 0.1 MPa on the exposed front face |
| Ring 1 supports | Shell end at x = 500 fixed axially (shell bending decays within 4√(R t) = 250); u_z = 0 on the symmetry plane |
| Thrust ring loads | Each saddle seat loaded with F/4 as a Hertz-like pressure p0 √(1 − (φ/20°)²) about the seat bottom |
| Thrust ring supports | Compression-only springs on the front-face contact annulus (shield end k = E/L = 156 N/mm³; casing end 400 N/mm³), iterated until no spring is in tension |
| Stress classification | Linearization along stress classification lines (ASME VIII-2 Annex 5-A): membrane Pm = average of each stress component along the line, bending Pb = its linear part; von Mises of each |
| Singular points | Weld roots and the 0.4 mm pipe-to-hole gap behind the front pipe weld are crack-like corners; their linear-elastic stress grows without limit as the mesh is refined, so they are reported but assessed by the weld line method and by linearization |
| Cross-check | Ring 1 web: FEA Pm+Pb vs the stepped-plate hand model (chapter 02). Thrust ring: FEA vs the curved-grid model in thrust-ring.md |
