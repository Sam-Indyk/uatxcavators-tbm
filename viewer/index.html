      Pm + Pb: membrane plus bending stress, the average and the linear part of the stress through a section (linearized stress). It is compared with yield; peaks at sharp re-entrant corners such as weld roots and the 0.4 mm pipe-to-hole gap grow without limit with mesh refinement and are reported separately. Von Mises stress: the single equivalent stress compared with the yield strength.
