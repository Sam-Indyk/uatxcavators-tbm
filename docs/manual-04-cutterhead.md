Σ r = 4,473.9 mm. With the stall torque shared equally by force, each bit carries F_t = T / Σr = 11.58e6 / 4,474 = 2.59 kN tangential; at LC3 each carries F/24 = 33.4 kN axial. A single bit that catches an obstruction while the motor stalls carries F_t = T/R: 11.58e6 / 296 = 39.1 kN at the outermost single track. That sizes the holder weld (CH-7): 14 mm fillet all round. The weld group of a 30 × 24 holder with the force along the 24 side, 31 above the arm face:

```
A_w = 2 (b + d) · a,   S_w = (b d + d²/3) · a,   a = 0.707 × 14,  b = 30, d = 24
f = √[(F_t / A_w)² + (31 F_t / S_w)²]
```

The three gauge holders at R308.65 overhang the arm end (R310) by 13.65 of their 30 mm length; only 16.35 is welded. That holder does not meet the check (CH-7, gauge) and is an open item for the cutterhead layout.

## 4.3 Arms and gussets

Thrust reaches the hub through 8 arm + gusset T-sections. At LC3 each arm carries its share of F plus the jam-moment share at the bit centroid R_c = 238:

```
F_arm = F/8 + 2M/(8 R_c);    M_arm = F_arm (R_c − 165);    σ = M_arm c / I   (T-section: arm 76 × 50.8 + gusset 25 × 125)
gusset-to-arm fillets: q = V Q / I,  τ = q / (2 × 0.707 × 12)
```

## 4.4 Head joint on the spindle

The locking assembly carries the torque; the head moment is carried by a couple between the locking-assembly zone and the journal sleeve's rear bore fit, 139 apart (SP-5). The couple pressure under the locking assembly stays below its grip pressure on the shaft (SP-6), so the joint does not open. Head thrust does not pass through the locking assembly: it goes by contact from the hub ring into the journal sleeve and on to the front 23228 inner ring (CH-8).

Head removal: centre cap off (2 × M12 pull holes) → release the 12 locking-assembly screws → M30 push-off screw against the spindle nose.

Equations behind the rows:

- **CH-1 torque**: rating 28.16 kNm (catalogue) → **20.00 kNm**
- **CH-2 geometry**: D_N = D √[(Fy + p C)/(Fy − p C)] = 190 √[(345 + 165)/(345 − 165)] = 320; fitted 340 → **319.82 mm**
- **CH-3 LC3**: F_arm = F/8 + 2M/(8 R_c) = 192 kN; M = F_arm (238 − 165); σ = M c/I, I = 1.82e+07 → **85.15 MPa**
- **CH-4 LC3**: q = V Q/I = 191580 × 151810/1.82e+07; τ = q/(2 × 0.707 × 12) → **93.95 MPa**
- **CH-3 LC4**: F_arm = F/8 + 2M/(8 R_c) = 215 kN; M = F_arm (238 − 165); σ = M c/I, I = 1.82e+07 → **95.69 MPa**
- **CH-4 LC4**: q = V Q/I = 215287 × 151810/1.82e+07; τ = q/(2 × 0.707 × 12) → **105.58 MPa**
- **CH-5 torque**: σ = F_t (238 − 165)/S, F_t = T/(8 × 238) → **12.38 MPa**
- **CH-6 LC3**: σ = 3(3 + ν)P/(8π t²), P = 7/24 F → **102.20 MPa**
- **CH-7 jam**: F_t = T/R = 11.58e6/295.95 = 39.1 kN; M = 31 F_t; f = √[(F_t/A_w)² + (M/S_w)²], A_w = 1069, S_w = 9027 → **139.26 MPa**. a bit nearer the centre sees F_t = T/R: an obstruction there is limited by what the obstruction can carry
- **CH-7 jam**: F_t = T/R = 11.58e6/308.65 = 37.5 kN; M = 31 F_t; f = √[(F_t/A_w)² + (M/S_w)²], A_w = 799, S_w = 5784 → **206.46 MPa**. gauge holder overhangs the arm: open item (cutterhead layout)
- **CH-8 LC3**: σ = F/[π(82.5² − 73²)] → **172.51 MPa**
