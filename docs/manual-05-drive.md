Selection:

```
Nominal:  T_N · S_t = 11.58 kNm (stall at the relief) × 1.4 (temperature factor, ≤ 60 °C) = 16.21 kNm ≤ T_KN 24.0
Maximum:  T_d · S_t = 20 × 1.4 = 28 kNm ≤ T_Kmax 48
Bore:     hub 1 holds the Ø180 locking assembly → max finish bore ≥ 180 → ROTEX 160 (185); ROTEX 140 stops at 160
```

Why a flexible coupling and not a rigid splined sleeve. The spindle and the motor shaft each run in their own bearings. Under the jam moment the spindle bends between its bearings and its rear end tilts:

```
θ = M L / (6 E I) = 87.9e6 × 400 / (6 × 200,000 × 1.89e+07) = 1.55e-03 rad   (LC3)
offset at the coupling centre (x = 757.5): δ = θ × (757.5 − 514) = 0.38 mm
```

The spindle's overhang behind the rear bearing has a stiffness k = 3EI / (a² (L + a)) = 297 kN/mm, so forcing a rigid connection through that offset would put about 112 kN into the motor's own bearings. The ROTEX takes the offset in its spider (0.46 mm capacity; check DR-7). A one-piece splined sleeve was considered at the start of Rev I and dropped for this reason.

Assembly: hub 1 goes in through the Ø300 rear bore before the motor, and its 10 locking-assembly screws are tightened through the Ø190 spider bore before the spider goes in. The motor arrives with the pilot plate and hub 2 already fitted.

## 5.5 Guards

Rules 3.a.1: rotating parts guarded. Two curved 3 mm steel plates cover the windows (x = 649-817, ±29° about 3 and 9 o'clock), each held by 4 × M6 × 12 into tapped holes in the adapter tube. With the guards on, the coupling is enclosed by the adapter, the pilot plate and the motor.
