Two spherical roller bearings 400 apart carry all radial load and the jam moment as a couple; the 29328N carries all thrust. All three are self-aligning, so the spindle may tilt under the jam moment without edge-loading a bearing. The 29328N housing washer sits radially free in a Ø241 bore, so it takes no radial load. End play 0.10-0.20 is set by shims under the seal carrier.

Thrust path through the module: journal sleeve (head) → front 23228 inner ring → collar → backing ring → 29328N → tube shoulder → tube → tube land → split flange → ring 1 seat. The inner ring of the front 23228 works as a spacer (face stress row BR-6).

### Bearing loads

The jam moment M is reacted by a radial couple on the two 23228s; the side force F_s, applied at the cutting face a = 329 ahead of the front bearing centre, adds a reaction at 90° to it:

```
R_M = M / L = M / 400                    R_s = F_s (L + a) / L = F_s × 1.823
F_r,front = √(R_M² + R_s²)               s0 = C0 / F_r          (F_a on the 23228s = 0: the 29328N takes it)
29328N: s0 = C0 / F                       L10 = (C / P)^(10/3) million revolutions, P = F at LC1
```

Load path in the flange: the tube land's rear face (x = 15, R148-159) pushes the flange lip; the flange's rear face bears on the ring 1 seat from R161 to R188. The lip root shears at R159.2 over the lip length 15; the flange body bends between the lip (R153.6) and the seat centroid (FL-4, a strip model that ignores the ring's hoop stiffness and so overstates it).

Seat clamp. The seat stays closed while the compression from F exceeds the tension the jam moment would cause on the far side:

```
N_needed = M · A / S − F,   A = π(R_cb² − 161.25²),  S = π(R_cb⁴ − 161.25⁴)/(4 R_cb)
```

At every case N_needed is far below the 16 × M12 clamp; thrust keeps the seat closed by itself whenever F > M·A/S.

Torque path. 20 kNm passes tube → 4 pins → flange → ring 1 by the friction of the 16 × M12 clamp (μ 0.30 clean: FoS 2.08; μ 0.15 oily: 1.04), with the 2 dowels as backup after any slip (FL-8). Under thrust the seat friction adds μ F r ≈ 0.15 × 613 kN × 0.18 m = 16.6 kNm more, not counted.

Bending is highest at the front bearing: the full jam moment passes the head joint and reaches the front 23228, then falls linearly to zero at the rear 23228. The spindle rotates under that moment, so the bending stress reverses every revolution; at LC1 the fillet at the collar is checked for infinite life (SP-4):

```
σ_a = Kt · 32 M / (π d³),  Kt = 1.6 (r 2 fillet, D/d = 165/140)
S_e = 0.5 Fu · k_surface · k_size · k_reliability = 0.5 × 860 × 0.80 × 0.75 × 0.81
```

A jam stops the motor, so the LC2-LC4 moments do not rotate with the shaft for more than a fraction of a revolution; they are static checks (SP-1, SP-2).
