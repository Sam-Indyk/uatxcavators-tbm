## 8.3 Open items

1. Poclain certified drawing: flange pattern (8 × M20 on Ø335 assumed), pilot, shaft length (110 assumed), body. Sets the pilot plate, hub 2 length and the motor envelope.
2. Fenner: B-LOC B400 130 × 180 hub pressure (160 MPa assumed, scaled from the 140 row) for the hub 1 check (DR-10).
3. KTR: ROTEX 160 hubs shortened (hub 1 body 60 with Ø180 H7, 42CrMo4+QT; hub 2 jaw disc 30 + Ø140 neck with W90 × 3 × 28 spline). Confirm with KTR.
4. Koyo: C0, C and misalignment limits for 23228 and 29328N; 23228 quantity (front, rear, spare).
5. Ring 1 plate: mill certificate for 4.5 in A572 Gr 42 (Fy ≥ 290).
6. Shell pipe grade (API 5L B / A53 B assumed, Fy 241).
7. Cartridge tube: US source for seamless 323.9 × 60 (OD ≥ 320, ID ≤ 206).
8. Gauge bit holders (R308.65) overhang the arm end by 13.65 of 30: holder or arm-tip change with the cutterhead layout (CH-7).
9. Centre cap and inner bit layout (7 bits on the cap).
10. Slurry hose connection at the pipe ends (x = 1,255): fitting OD ≤ 95 to pass inside the jacking pipes later.
11. Casing joint drawing: confirm the register placeholders (spigot Ø589.5 × 10 × 9 wide, shield rear counterbore Ø590.6 × 12) and the casing wall (24 in × 0.375 assumed).
12. Soil c_u from the geotechnical report: fins give FoS 1.09 alone at c_u 25 kPa (FN-1).
13. Launch seal notches for the Ø750 fin envelope.
14. Overall length 1,451 vs the 1,340 envelope from Rev F: accept or change the motor arrangement.
15. Ring 1 FEA mesh refinement study on the pipe-hole wall (one more level, h 14 → 10).
