Equations behind the rows:

- **LF-1 lift**: P = m_pt g / sin 60° = 306 kg × 9.81/0.866 = 3.46 kN = 353 kg-f; WLL 2150 kg (5:1) → **352.99 kg**. FoS on breaking load = 5 × 6.1
- **LF-2 lift**: P = m g/2 = 1206 × 9.81/2 = 5.91 kN = 603 kg-f → **602.75 kg**
- **LF-2b lift**: 700 kg marked vs 603 kg → **602.75 kg**
- **LF-3 lift**: τ = P/(0.5 π d L_e) = 6867/(0.5π × 20 × 31.9) → **6.85 MPa**
- **LF-4 lift**: τ = P/(π D t) = 6867/(π × 60 × 12.7) → **2.87 MPa**
- **LF-5 lift**: σ ≈ β P/t² = 1.5 × 6867/12.7²; FoS on ultimate = 414/64 = 6.5 → **63.86 MPa**. FoS on yield ≥ 3 and on ultimate ≥ 5 for the welded lifting attachment
- **LF-6 lift**: m = 325 kg; DIN 580 M30 WLL 3,200 kg (axial) → **325.20 kg**


## 7.4 Transport and handling (3 g)

The cantilevered module, head and drive hang off ring 1 at the seat. Their weight moment there is 3.05 kNm at 1 g, 9.1 kNm at 3 g:

```
M12 flange screw tension added at 3 g:  ΔF = 4M / (n D) = 4 × 9.1e6 / (16 × 354) = 6.5 kN   vs preload 49.0 kN
Weight shear at 3 g vs flange friction:  3 × 748 × 9.81 = 22.0 kN   vs 0.30 × 16 × 49.0 = 235 kN
Moment at 3 g vs the jam moment the joint is designed for: 9.1 vs 87.5 kNm
```
